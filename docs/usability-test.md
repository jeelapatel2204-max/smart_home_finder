# Buyer decision-workflow test

## Goal

Learn whether a prospective buyer can use Smart Home Finder to narrow a shortlist with confidence.

## Participants

Recruit five people actively considering a home purchase. Include first-time buyers where possible. Do not collect financial-account details or use their real addresses during the session.

## Session flow

1. Ask the participant to find homes in a market they recognize.
2. Ask them to enter a few requirements using Must Have, Prefer, and No Preference.
3. Ask them to explain why a displayed home is a match or near match.
4. Ask them to compare two or three homes and choose one to investigate further.
5. Ask what information they trusted, what felt unclear, and what they expected to happen next.

## Observe

- Time to create a usable profile.
- Whether participants understand the difference between Must Have and Prefer.
- Whether True Monthly Cost changes their shortlist.
- Whether they can explain a match score without assistance.
- Whether they find the comparison table sufficient for a next action.

## Success signals

At least four of five participants complete profile → evaluate → compare without help, correctly identify a tradeoff, and say the monthly-cost or match explanation influenced their shortlist.

## Record

Capture consented notes only. Use anonymous participant IDs and summarize recurring issues by severity. Feed confirmed findings into [decisions.md](./decisions.md) before beginning backend work.
