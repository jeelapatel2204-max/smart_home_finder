## Slide 2 — The problem

# Home search helps people browse. It does not help them decide.

- Buyers jump between listings, mortgage calculators, maps, notes, and agent messages.
- The list price hides the real monthly commitment: taxes, insurance, HOA, maintenance, deposits, and renter fees.
- A home can look perfect online but fail a buyer’s budget, commute, or future needs.
- People make one of the biggest financial decisions of their lives with disconnected tools.

**Bottom line:** Home search is an information problem; choosing a home is a decision problem.

---

## Slide 3 — The solution

# One home page. A clearer decision.

Smart Home Finder turns every listing into a personal decision workspace.

- **Decision Dossier:** all-in cost, cash to close, reserves, stress tests, offer guardrails, and pre-offer checks.
- **Lifestyle fit:** neighborhood signals, nearby essentials, commute/destination planning, and daily-life context.
- **Rental clarity:** base rent plus adjustable renter’s insurance, pet fees, and home-care reserve.
- **Next actions:** saved homes, comparison, sharing, and assigned-agent tour requests.

**Visual:** Four annotated product screenshots: dossier, destination route planner, rental affordability, comparison.

## Slide 5 — Why now

# Buyers need more confidence, not more tabs.

- Housing costs make monthly affordability and cash reserves more important than headline price alone.
- Consumers increasingly expect personalized, interactive guidance while they shop.
- Agents need better ways to qualify serious buyers and guide them toward a decision.
- Public map and routing infrastructure makes useful location context possible before expensive enterprise data integrations.

**Opportunity:** Become the trusted decision layer between property discovery and an offer or lease.

---

## Slide 6 — Business model

# Free for shoppers. Valuable when a decision becomes actionable.

### Phase 1 — Consumer acquisition

- Free home search, decision dossiers, comparison, and planning tools.

### Phase 2 — Agent and brokerage tools

- Paid agent profiles and qualified tour/lead workflow.
- Brokerage workspace for branded decision dossiers and buyer collaboration.

### Phase 3 — Marketplace revenue

- Referral revenue from lenders, renters insurance, moving, and home services.
- Premium data and decision-support tools for serious buyers and investors.

**Important:** Validate each monetization channel with pilots before forecasting revenue.

---

## Slide 7 — Go-to-market

# Start locally, prove engagement, then expand.

1. Launch in one metro with a small group of buyer-friendly agents.
2. Give agents a branded link they can use with active clients.
3. Measure dossiers created, homes compared, tours requested, and buyer conversion.
4. Use the pilot case study to sign brokerages and add additional metros.

### First ideal customers

- First-time buyers overwhelmed by affordability.
- Renters deciding between several apartments.
- Independent agents who want a better buyer consultation experience.

## Presenter notes — keep the pitch credible

- Lead with the working product, but say clearly that listings and agent records are currently sample data.
- Never say “AI-powered” as the core claim until a real model/provider is connected. Today, describe the free Smart Home Advisor as property-aware decision guidance.
- Do not claim Zillow, Google Maps, MLS, or agent partnerships unless contracts or permissions are in place.
- Your best proof points will be pilot engagement: completed dossiers, comparisons, tours, and agent feedback.
